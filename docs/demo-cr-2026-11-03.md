# Demo: late and in-week menu publishing (CR 2026-11-03)

Setup: `pnpm db:local`, `pnpm db:seed`, `pnpm dev` with `CLOCK_FAKE_ENABLED=true`, then sign in as the HC admin (PNJP000003) and an employee (PNJP000001). Move the fake clock from `/system`.

## 1. A menu published inside its own week has no forecast

1. Set the fake time to Wednesday 10:00 of week N, where week N's menu has not been published yet.
2. HC admin, `/admin/menu`, week N: the header reads "Công bố trong tuần phục vụ: không có dự trù…" with the "Công bố trong tuần" badge. Publish.
3. The week shows as "Đã chốt dự trù" straight away. The forecast export for week N is all zeros.
4. Employee, `/week`, week N: the banner says there is no forecast. "Đăng ký thực tế" opens first; Monday to Wednesday are locked (08:00 has passed), and Thursday and Friday stay editable until their own 08:00. "Đăng ký dự trù" shows the empty table with the same note.
5. Reconciliation for Thursday shows every meal as a difference from a forecast of 0.

Automated: `MenuTest.inWeekPublish`; rules in `CloseAtTest` ("BR-04: publishing week 45's menu on Wednesday of week 45 has no forecast", "…at 00:00 Monday of week N…").

## 2. A late publish before the week starts takes the HC admin's closeAt

1. Set the fake time to Saturday 09:30 of week N-1, with week N still unpublished.
2. `/admin/menu`, week N: the "Công bố trễ: tự chọn hạn đăng ký" badge and the "Hạn đăng ký dự trù (đến hết)" field, prefilled with Sunday 09:29 (publish + `registration.latePublishHours`). The field does not go past Sunday 23:59.
3. Change it to Saturday 16:59 and publish: the confirm dialog and then the header show "Hạn đăng ký: đến hết … 16:59".
4. At 17:00 the close-week job (or the next registration request) takes the forecast snapshot as usual.

Automated: `MenuTest.latePublish` (missing closeAt, a closeAt in the past and Monday 00:01 are refused with 400 on `closeAt`); `CloseAtTest` "BR-04: the closeAt the HC admin picks…".
