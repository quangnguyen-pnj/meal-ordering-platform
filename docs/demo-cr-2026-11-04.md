# Demo: fallback meal unit (CR 2026-11-04)

Setup: `pnpm db:local`, `pnpm db:seed`, `pnpm dev`, then sign in as the HC admin (PNJP000003). The seed sets no fallback meal unit. Kho vận (KHO) has no meal unit above it, and Xưởng Sửa chữa (XSC) is a meal unit with no floor; both have the Gò Vấp canteen from the root.

## 1. Without a fallback, nothing changes

1. `/admin/org`, tab "Chưa đăng ký cơm được": KHO and XSC employees are listed with "…: chưa đặt cơm được".
2. Sign in as a KHO employee: `/week` shows "Phòng ban của bạn chưa được thiết lập nhà ăn nên chưa thể đặt cơm…".

## 2. The HC admin picks a fallback meal unit

1. `/admin/org`, select "Tổ Bảo vệ" (a valid meal unit, floor T1). Under "Đầu mối nhận cơm tập trung", tick "Dùng đơn vị này làm đầu mối nhận cơm tập trung". A toast confirms and offers undo; the tree shows the "Đầu mối tập trung" badge.
2. Select Kho vận: "Hiện trạng" says its employees have no meal unit and their meals go to the fallback Tổ Bảo vệ, at T1.
3. Tab "Chưa đăng ký cơm được": KHO and XSC employees now read "…: vẫn đặt cơm được, nhận tại đầu mối tập trung".
4. Ticking the box on XSC (no floor) or trying it on a node that is not a meal unit is refused. A meal unit with a floor but no canteen is accepted: the fallback takes meals of every canteen (2026-11-05).
   - On a fallback with a floor but no canteen, the tree badge stays grey ("Đầu mối nhận cơm · N người" + "Đầu mối tập trung"), "Chỉ xem đầu mối nhận cơm" shows "Mọi nhà ăn" and "Hợp lệ", and "Hiện trạng" says it needs no canteen. Its own employees whose department has a canteen order there normally; those whose department has none still cannot.
5. Ticking it on another meal unit (e.g. Phòng IT) moves it: Tổ Bảo vệ loses the badge. Both changes are in the audit log (`orgUnit.mapping.updated`).

Automated: `OrgTest.fallbackMealUnit`; rules in `MappingTest` "BR-60/BR-63 (CR 2026-11-04): fallback meal unit".

## 3. The employee registers; the forecast counts them at the fallback

1. Sign in as a KHO employee: `/week` shows the Gò Vấp menu; register a few meals.
2. After closeAt (or with the fake clock), the forecast lines of that employee carry the fallback meal unit and its floor (`ForecastLine.mealUnitId`, `floorId`).
3. Give KHO a meal unit (tick "Có" on Kho vận with a floor): days not yet snapshotted follow Kho vận; lines already written stay at the fallback (BR-64).

Automated: `RegistrationTest` "BR-60 (CR 2026-11-04): once a fallback meal unit is set, an employee with no meal unit registers" and the snapshot test (line at `ou_BV`, `fl_T1`).

## 4. A department without a canteen still cannot register

Only an employee whose department resolves no canteen is blocked; they see the message asking them to contact the administration staff. Automated: `MappingTest` "BR-63: an employee whose department has no canteen cannot register".
